## 3. Model fits: `em ~ structure * fusion`

### 3a. Logistic regression, independence assumed (3320 = 4 x 830 rows)

| term | coef | SE | z | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Intercept | -0.7131 | 0.0739 | -9.652 | 4.8e-22 |
| structure | 0.0594 | 0.1040 | 0.572 | 0.567 |
| fusion | 0.1231 | 0.1035 | 1.189 | 0.234 |
| **structure:fusion** | **0.0797** | **0.1454** | **0.548** | **0.583** |

Model LLR p = 0.064 — the four cells are not jointly distinguishable at 0.05.

### 3b. GEE, binomial logit, exchangeable working correlation, cluster = `instance_id` — **PREFERRED**

| term | coef | robust SE | z | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Intercept | -0.7131 | 0.0739 | -9.652 | 4.8e-22 |
| structure | 0.0594 | 0.0863 | 0.689 | 0.491 |
| fusion | 0.1231 | 0.0887 | 1.388 | 0.165 |
| **structure:fusion** | **0.0797** | **0.1167** | **0.683** | **0.494** |

Estimated within-instance (exchangeable) correlation: **rho = 0.3329**. 830 clusters, cluster size
exactly 4.

**Why GEE is preferred:** the four cells are repeated measures on the same 830 questions, so the
4 x 830 rows are not independent — an instance that is easy is easy under all four conditions
(rho = 0.33 confirms substantial within-instance dependence). The naive logit's SEs are computed
as if all 3320 rows were independent, which is the wrong variance for a within-subject factorial.
GEE with an exchangeable working correlation and cluster-robust (sandwich) SEs gives
population-averaged coefficients on the same scale as the logit but with variance that accounts
for the clustering. Point estimates are identical (same GLM mean structure); only the SEs differ,
and here the cluster-robust SEs are *tighter* — pairing on instances buys precision on the
within-instance contrasts. **Both fits agree: the interaction is not significant.**

## 5. IDENTIFICATION AUDIT — the 2x2 is **NOT identified**

The reviewer's caveat is correct on both counts, and there is a third.

### 5a. Answer-format coaching: perfectly collinear with `structure`

Rendered by re-composing each condition's system prompt with the project's own
`agent_search.prompts.loader.load_condition(..., domain="general", profile="browsecomp")` —
the exact call path `evaluation` uses.

| cell | structure | renders a tool manual? | which | mean `initial_prompt_tokens` over its 830 rows |
|---|:---:|---|---|---:|
| neither (`bm25_fetch_snip`) | 0 | **NO** | — | **985** |
| fusion alone (`hybrid_fetch_snip`) | 0 | **NO** | — | **1024** |
| structure alone (`snip`) | 1 | **YES** | `search_s` -> `skills/bql_browsecomp.md` | **3019** |
| both / Sieve (`bql_dense_snip`) | 1 | **YES** | `search_bqlds` -> `skills/bql_browsecomp.md` | **3023** |

So `structure = 1` <=> "renders `bql_browsecomp.md`", exactly. The two coached cells carry
~2,000 extra system-prompt tokens the two uncoached cells never see.

That manual is not only query syntax. Lines matched in the composed prompt that bear directly on
**exact match**, the outcome metric:

- "The answer is **the shortest span COPIED VERBATIM from the slice** — exact spelling and
  accents, and just the"
- "**asked-for unit, not a compound** (the year alone, not \"March 2009\")."
- "document (a different search hit, or a new search), **never fill the gap from memory**."
- "entity both 0-hit? PIVOT to a different entity the question names; **never answer from
  memory**."

plus the stopping rule "Once a slice shows a fact of the right type, ANSWER — don't re-search to
confirm." An instruction to emit the shortest verbatim span and only the asked-for unit is, on an
EM metric, a direct scoring intervention. The paper already states this confound in
`latex/sections/experimental_setup.tex` (para. "The conditions differ in coaching as well as
interface") — what it does not state is that the confound applies to the 2x2 grid as well, which
is the point here.

*(Provenance note: the two uncoached cells' `config.json` `prompt_sha256` still matches the
prompt composed from today's tree byte-for-byte (`10f01435a7f773dd`, `5730222ac885b204`). The two
coached cells' do not (`9b61845e5c810125` -> `7e3b1e2c387805a5`; `03b6d514e44c0002` ->
`ad69f699648c1604`), i.e. `bql_browsecomp.md` has been edited since those runs. The manual was
nonetheless present at run time and about the same size: `initial_prompt_tokens` of ~3020 vs
~1000 for the uncoached cells is the manual, measured on the runs themselves.)*

### 5b. Retrieval pool size: also perfectly collinear with `structure`

Measured two ways — the tool schema's advertised default, and the depth actually rendered in
every one of the ~120,000 search observations in these four cells (parsed from the listing
header, which stamps the returned depth; see the script for every header shape).

| cell | structure | search tool | schema default k | mean listing depth actually shown | modal depth | search calls | calls widened past default |
|---|:---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| neither | 0 | `bm25_search_snip` | **10** | **9.90** | 10 (30,524) | 31,395 | 2 (0.01%) |
| fusion alone | 0 | `hybrid_search_snip` | **10** | **10.00** | 10 (23,163) | 23,281 | 0 (0.00%) |
| structure alone | 1 | `search_s` | **5** | **4.51** | 5 (24,858) | 33,937 | 1,221 (3.60%) |
| both / Sieve | 1 | `search_bqlds` | **5** | **4.57** | 5 (23,532) | 31,924 | 1,296 (4.06%) |

(The sub-5 mean in the structure cells is because a BQL filter often matches fewer than 5
documents; the structure cells' depth histogram has real mass at 1-4. The `_TOPK` env knobs in
these runs' sidecars are `BM25_FETCH_TOPK=10`, `DENSE_FETCH_TOPK=10`, and `HYBRID_FETCH_TOPK`
unset i.e. its default 10; the BQL path does not read a `_TOPK` knob at all — its depth is the
`args.get("k", 5)` default in `DocSearchFetch.run`.)

So the structure = 0 cells show the agent **twice as many candidates per search call** as the
structure = 1 cells. This matches, and confirms with run-level evidence, the paper's own
`experimental_setup.tex` statement that pool size is k=5 for Sieve and its ablation rungs and
k=10 for the four fetch-family controls.

*(Minor factual note for the paper, not load-bearing here: `experimental_setup.tex` says "the BM25
and dense tools ignore a requested `k` by construction". `Bm25FetchSnipWorkspace.run` and
`HybridFetchSnipWorkspace.run` both do honour a requested `k`
(`int(k) if k else None` -> `self.bm.search(query, k=k or self.topk)`), and the neither-cell's
observations contain one depth-50 and one depth-20 listing as a result. It happened on 9 of
31,395 calls, so it changes nothing quantitatively.)*

### 5c. A third asymmetry the brief did not raise: `fusion` is not the same manipulation at the two levels of `structure`

- At **structure = 0**, fusion = global RRF (k=60) over **two independent top-100 pools**
  (`HybridFetchSnipWorkspace.search` + `rrf_fuse`). Dense **can introduce documents BM25 never
  ranked** — it changes the candidate set, i.e. it is recall-expanding.
- At **structure = 1**, fusion = RRF (k=60) of BM25 order against dense order **restricted to the
  BQL filter-passing candidate ids** (`dense_fuse.py`, `DenseBelief.score(..., doc_ids=candidates)`,
  never `top_k_doc_ids`). Dense **can never introduce a document**, only reorder — and on the
  0-exact-hit coverage fallback it can only reorder *within* a coverage tier.

So factor B is "add a recall-expanding dense channel" at one level of A and "reorder the existing
candidates by dense similarity" at the other. That is a milder version of exactly the flaw that
made v1 uninterpretable. It does not have a fix within the runs that exist.

### 5d. Verdict

**The 2x2 is NOT identified.** `structure` is perfectly collinear with (a) rendering an
EM-relevant answer-format coaching manual and (b) halving the retrieval pool depth from 10 to 5;
and `fusion` is not even the same operation at the two levels of `structure`. No coefficient in
Section 3 — main effect or interaction — can be attributed to the structured query surface. The
grid would need an arm that does not exist: Sieve stripped of its manual (or a baseline given a
neutral answer-format-only manual), at matched k.

Note that the two confounds do not even point the same way: coaching favours the structure cells,
while the k=5 vs k=10 pool favours the non-structure cells. That does not rescue anything — two
uncontrolled factors of unknown magnitude in opposite directions is not a control.

## 6. What this means for the paper

The abstract currently reads: *"Its components are not additive: dense fusion alone hurts,
significantly so uncorrected, and pays only alongside the structured surface (interaction
p=1.8e-4)."* Both halves of that sentence trace to v1's wrong (0,1) cell:

- "**dense fusion alone hurts**" (27.7 vs 32.9, the figure also carried in `latex_acl8/main.tex`
  as "$27.7$ vs.\ $32.9$ EM at a matched read interface, $p{=}0.00893$ raw") is the **dense-only**
  cell, i.e. dense *replacing* BM25, not dense *added to* it. With dense actually added to BM25 at
  the same read interface, fusion alone **helps** (35.66 vs 32.89, +2.77, McNemar p=0.185 — a
  directional gain, not a significant loss).
- "**interaction p=1.8e-4**" is v1's GEE interaction on that same wrong cell. The corrected GEE
  interaction is **p=0.494**.

`latex_acl8/main.tex`'s limitation (iii) explicitly says "The structure x fusion interaction
itself (p=1.8e-4, GEE clustered on instance) is *not* retracted." On this evidence it should be.

**One plain sentence: the paper may claim no structure x dense-fusion interaction in any form —
with the correct BM25-plus-dense-RRF cell in the (0,1) corner the interaction is not significant
(GEE p=0.494, logit p=0.583, raw contrast +1.9 EM), and even that null is uninterpretable as an
interaction *of structure* because the structure factor is perfectly collinear with the BQL
manual's answer-format coaching and with a 10-to-5 halving of the retrieval pool.**

What *does* survive untouched by 5a/5b is the single-axis rung of gate 1: Sieve vs its own
no-dense-evidence twin (`bql_dense_snip` vs `snip`), +4.70 EM, p=0.0104 on
BrowseComp-Plus-Structured. Those two cells render the **same** manual and run at the **same**
k=5, so that contrast varies only the dense channel — it is the one dense-fusion claim in the
paper that this audit does not disturb. Gate 2 (Sieve @cap100 vs the hybrid control on HotpotQA,
+5.84 EM, p=2.8e-29) reproduces exactly as published, but note it is a *coached-vs-uncoached,
k=5-vs-k=10* contrast, so it carries both confounds of 5a and 5b — as the paper's own
`experimental_setup.tex` already says of the same-interface controls.
