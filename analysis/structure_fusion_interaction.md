## 0. A correction to the brief before anything else

This task was briefed with approximate Appendix G numbers: "neither ~29.6 baseline, structure-alone
~+1.9 (n.s.), fusion-alone ~+1.9 (n.s.), both ~38.9 (+9.3, p=2.8e-6)". Those four numbers are real
— they are in `latex/sections/appendix.tex`'s Table~\ref{tab:factorial-grid} — **but they are not a
matched-read-interface structure x fusion 2x2.** 29.6 and 38.9 are the *same* SEARCH-engine row
("Query surf. + dense (RRF)") read at its two *different* READ levels (visit vs. fetch+snippets);
the two "+1.9" figures belong to two *other* rows' own visit->fetch deltas ("Query surface (no
dense)": 32.3->34.2; "Hybrid sparse-dense RRF": 33.7->35.7). That set of four numbers is confounded
with the READ axis — exactly the trap the task spec warned about. It was **not** reused here.

Instead, this script builds the actual clean 2x2 the run directories on disk support: all four
cells held at the **same** READ interface (fetch+snippets, under `_headline_validation` — Sieve's
own snippet-listing + section-fetch interface), varying only (a) whether the query surface is the
structured `bql` condition and (b) whether a dense retrieval channel is fused in.

### 5b. Clustered/mixed model: GEE, exchangeable working correlation, cluster = instance_id

Chosen over `BinomialBayesMixedGLM` because it gives population-averaged coefficients directly
comparable to the naive logit's, with cluster-robust standard errors — the natural fit for "same
830 instances observed under 4 conditions."

| term | coef | robust SE | z | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Intercept | -0.7131 | 0.074 | -9.652 | <0.001 |
| structure | 0.0594 | 0.086 | 0.689 | 0.491 |
| fusion | -0.2458 | 0.092 | -2.677 | 0.0074 |
| **structure:fusion** | **0.4485** | **0.120** | **3.741** | **0.000183** |

Point estimates are identical to the naive fit (both are the same GLM mean structure); the GEE's
cluster-robust SEs are *tighter* here, not looser — the repeated-measures structure (each instance
seen 4 times) makes the interaction estimate *more* precise once the within-instance correlation is
modeled, not less. **The two models agree**: the interaction is significant in both (naive
p=0.00244, GEE p=0.000183), and GEE is the one to trust as the primary number given the shared
instances.

**This does NOT reproduce the brief's approximate "+9.3, p=2.8e-6."** That number belongs to a
*different* contrast — the same "both" cell (bql+dense+snip fetch, EM=38.9) against its own
*visit*-interface twin (bql+dense **visit**, EM=29.6), i.e. it is a READ-interface contrast, not a

2x2 tested here, the "neither" cell is bm25+snip fetch (EM=32.9, not 29.6), so the honest
neither-vs-both contrast is +6.0 points, p=0.0025 — still real and significant, just not the +9.3
figure from the brief.

## 7. Interpretive summary

- **The structure x fusion interaction is significant in both models** (naive logit p=0.00244; GEE
  cluster-robust p=0.000183), at a properly matched read interface (fetch+snippets, same step
  budget, same 830 instances).
- What the interaction means, precisely: on the logit scale, the joint (structure=1, fusion=1)
  effect exceeds what the sum of the two main effects predicts. Concretely here that means
  something sharper than "the two act synergistically from a null baseline": **fusion alone is
  significantly *harmful*** (coef -0.246, p=0.007 GEE; EM drops from 32.9 to 27.7) — dense evidence
  without the structured bql query surface appears to dilute/mislead rather than help. Structure
  alone is a small, non-significant lift (coef +0.059, p=0.49 GEE). Only when both are present does
  accuracy jump to the best cell in the grid (38.9%, +6.0 over the true fetch-interface baseline).
  This is squarely in favor of the paper's "delivered correctly is the operative qualifier" thesis,
  but the precise mechanism is that dense fusion is actively counterproductive *without* structure,
  not merely inert.
- Do **not** write this up as "structure only works with fusion" as a blanket claim — the cell
  means don't show that shape (structure alone is a mild positive, not a null). The correct
  one-sentence framing is: **dense fusion is harmful on its own at this interface, and only becomes
  a large net positive when paired with the structured query surface — a genuine, statistically
  significant interaction, not a sum of two null main effects.**

## 8. Bottom line for the paper

**The paper may now claim a significant structure x dense-fusion interaction on
BrowseComp-Plus-Structured at the matched fetch+snippets read interface** (GEE-clustered p=0.000183,
naive p=0.00244, n=830, same step budget). It should **not** claim the specific "+9.3, p=2.8e-6"
figure as the interaction evidence — that number is a different (READ-axis) contrast already
reported in Appendix G's factorial grid. The interaction-supporting contrast from this fixed-interface
2x2 is: both=38.9% vs neither=32.9% (McNemar +6.0, p=0.0025), with fusion-alone significantly
*below* baseline (27.7%) and structure-alone a non-significant lift (34.2%) — i.e., a real,
non-additive interaction whose story is "fusion actively hurts without structure, and only helps
paired with it," which the paper should state in exactly those terms rather than as a simple additive
synergy.
