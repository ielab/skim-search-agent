## Corpus-vs-interface decomposition (extension)

For each dataset: flat-corpus baseline EM (**corpus step start**) → structured-corpus baseline EM
(same structure-blind agent, structured corpus — **the corpus step**) → structured-corpus full
method EM (bql+dense+snip fetch — **the interface step**). The interface-step numbers are
recomputed directly from `rows.jsonl` here (not copied), and match `comparison_result.md` /
`analysis/round5_analyses.py`'s M3 headline-family deltas exactly, as a consistency check.

**Assumption flagged, not tested:** "Δ total" = Δ corpus + Δ interface (additive). The full method
has never been run on the flat corpus, so no cell directly measures whether the corpus and
interface effects interact; if they do, this additive total could be wrong in either direction.
Treated here as an estimate under an untested assumption, not an observed quantity.

| dataset | flat-corpus baseline EM% | structured-corpus baseline EM% | Δ corpus (pp) | p (corpus) | structured-corpus full-method EM% | Δ interface (pp) | p (interface) | Δ total (pp) | corpus share | interface share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| browsecomp_plus_structured | 34.8 | 36.4 | +1.6 | 0.372 | 38.9 | +2.5 | 0.217 | +4.1 | 38% | 62% |
| hotpotqa_structured | 42.4 | 43.7 | +1.3 | **0.00978** | 45.3 | +1.6 | **0.00186** | +2.8 | 45% | 55% |
| musique_structured | 25.3 | 26.4 | +1.2 | 0.132 | 29.2 | +2.7 | **0.00073** | +3.9 | 30% | 70% |

**Cross-dataset pattern:** corpus share of the total ranges 30%–45% (browsecomp 38%, hotpotqa 45%,
musique 30%) — a 15-point spread. Whether that counts as "consistent" is a matter of framing: no
dataset gives the corpus effect close to 0% or close to 100% of the total, so the qualitative story
("both the corpus and the interface contribute; neither is negligible") holds on all three. But the
corpus's *individual* significance is dataset-dependent — significant alone only on hotpotqa,
directional-but-not-significant alone on browsecomp and musique — while the *interface* step is
significant on hotpotqa and musique but not browsecomp (its flagship, hardest benchmark). The
fairest single-sentence summary is **"corpus contributes roughly a third to a half of the total
gain on every dataset checked, but only reaches significance on its own on hotpotqa"** — not a
uniform "roughly half" rule, and not zero on any dataset.

## What hotpotqa shows, vs. the browsecomp pattern — DIFFERENT, not confirmatory

The task asked for an honest read either way. **Hotpotqa's isolation pattern is essentially the
inverse of browsecomp's**, on the two contrasts computable on both datasets:

- **Query surface alone**: decisive and highly significant on hotpotqa (+3.3 EM, p=3.6e-10, n=7343)
  vs. small and non-significant on browsecomp (+1.3 EM, p=0.531, n=830).
- **Cost of removing dense fusion** (full method vs. no-dense, i.e. dense fusion's contribution
  *on top of* the structured query surface): small and non-significant on hotpotqa (+0.8 EM,
  p=0.103) vs. large and significant on browsecomp (+4.7 EM, p=0.0104).
- (Browsecomp only, hotpotqa not yet computable) **dense retrieval alone, without the structured
  query surface**, actively *hurts* relative to plain BM25 on browsecomp (−5.2 EM, p=0.00893) —
  interpreted together with the above, dense fusion appears to help browsecomp only when paired
  with the structured query surface, not on its own.

Read together: on hotpotqa, the structured query surface (BQL) is doing essentially all of the
measurable work, and dense fusion adds little detectable value on top of it. On browsecomp, the
structured query surface alone is not doing detectable work, and dense fusion (in combination with
BQL) is the component supplying the significant lift. This is a genuinely **dataset-dependent
finding, not a replication** of the browsecomp story — worth stating plainly in the paper rather
than folding into a single "both components help" sentence. It is also consistent with a
plausible mechanism the paper can state as a hypothesis (not a proven cause): HotpotQA/MuSiQue are
Wikipedia multi-hop QA with fairly literal, field-structured entity/title matching where a
Boolean/field-tagged query surface has an easy, high-precision target; BrowseComp-Plus's harder,
more paraphrastic web-style questions may reward semantic (dense) matching more than exact
structured-field matching. The missing hotpotqa "dense alone" cell (currently 71.3% complete)
would directly test whether hotpotqa's dense-alone number is also weak (matching browsecomp's
sign) or actually helps once isolated from BQL — re-run `analysis/wiki_isolation.py` once that
cell crosses 90% to close this gap.

## Lift-able sentences (paper-ready, hedged)

**Job 1:**

> Across all three datasets, a structure-blind BM25 agent reading the structured corpus format
> outperforms the same agent reading a byte-differing flat corpus (browsecomp +1.6 EM pts,
> p=0.372; hotpotqa +1.3 pts, p<0.01; musique +1.2 pts, p=0.132) — directionally consistent on
> every dataset, individually significant only on hotpotqa at n=7342-7343.

> Decomposing each dataset's total gain (flat baseline → structured baseline → structured full
> method) into a corpus step and an interface step (additive estimate; the two effects have never
> been tested jointly since the full method has not been run on the flat corpus), the corpus
> accounts for 30-45% of the total across the three datasets (browsecomp 38%, hotpotqa 45%,
> musique 30%) and the interface for the remaining 55-70% — the corpus is a real, non-negligible
> contributor everywhere we can measure it, not a confound to explain away, but the paper's
> primary claim should rest on the interface step, which is the larger and (on 2 of 3 datasets)
> the more consistently significant of the two.

**Job 2:**

> Component-isolation contrasts computed on hotpotqa_structured (n=7343, fully released cells)
> show a pattern that inverts, rather than replicates, the same contrasts on
> browsecomp_plus_structured: the structured query surface alone drives a large, highly
> significant gain on hotpotqa (+3.3 EM pts, p=3.6e-10) but a small, non-significant one on

> This dataset-dependence suggests the two components' isolation results reported for
